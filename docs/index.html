On-device dictation for macOS

Voice,
given form.

Speak anywhere on your Mac. Murti carves your raw voice into finished text — English, Hindi, and Hinglish — entirely on your device.

Install with Homebrew View source
$ brew install technomad641/murti/murti

Finished text, not a transcript

Everything runs locally on the Apple Silicon GPU. No account, no upload.

🎙️

Dictate anywhere

Press ⌘⌥D, speak, press again. It types at your cursor in any app.

⚡

Instant when you stop

Transcribes while you speak, so stopping is near-instant — no wait that grows with how long you talked.

🌐

English · Hindi · Hinglish

True code-switching. Hindi comes out romanized by default, or Devanagari — your choice.

🧠

On-device

Whisper large-v3-turbo runs on your GPU via MLX. Private and offline.

⏸️

Pause & duck audio

Music and video auto-pause while you speak and resume after; other audio ducks out of your mic.

📖

Learns your words

An auto-learning dictionary remembers the names and jargon you dictate.

📈

Local insights

Words, words-per-minute, time saved and streaks — computed and stored on your Mac.

🔎

Inspect studio

Point Murti at any app you own and read its architecture from the shipped binary. No source.

☁️

Cloud preview

Meetings, accounts, team and sync are previewed in the app — the dictation core never needs them.

Three keystrokes

1

Click into any text field

A message, a doc, your editor — wherever the cursor is blinking.

2

Press ⌘⌥D and speak

Murti listens and transcribes on-device as you talk.

3

Press ⌘⌥D again

Your finished sentence lands right where the cursor was.

Install in one line

On an Apple Silicon Mac with Homebrew. The first launch sets up on-device models once; after that murti starts instantly.

$ brew install technomad641/murti/murti

Prefer source, or want the double-click app? See the install guide.

Your voice stays yours

Transcription runs entirely on-device — your audio is never uploaded for dictation. Settings, dictionary and insights live in ~/.config/murti, and the dashboard binds to 127.0.0.1 only.
